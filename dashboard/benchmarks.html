The performance lab.

How close can an idiomatic Kotlin server get to Rust/Axum? Our working target is at least 90% of Axum's throughput, with p99 latency and CPU cost within 10% at comparable successful load.

Benchmark methodology

Hosted CI runs are exploratory. Controlled comparisons use an independent load generator. The raw results and recorded conditions are linked from each run.
